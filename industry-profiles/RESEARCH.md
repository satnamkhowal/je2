# Industry profile editorial record

Reviewed: 23 September 2026. This expansion adds 100 people to the original eight profiles.

## Selection

The new profiles have a publicly listed current role at an established employer and an observed LinkedIn audience above 35,000. The directory covers software, data, AI, product, engineering leadership and career guidance. It does not assert that these people recently joined their employer. Founders with a separate qualifying day job can be included; an earlier employer alone is insufficient.

Current professional profiles, first-person announcements, employer biographies and personal professional sites were checked. Older creator-directory headlines were not treated as authoritative when newer career information contradicted them. Candidates with an unresolved current employer were omitted.

## Evidence and dates

`profiles.json` contains a direct LinkedIn URL, sources, a follower-source URL and review date for each new profile. Its experience entries link to career sources. Text is an editorial paraphrase, not a LinkedIn export. Learning-value descriptions are editorial interpretation rather than measured engagement statistics.

Follower counts are rounded public-source snapshots, not live API measurements. A review date records when the source was checked, not when a search engine or directory measured its count. Sources can lag. Role titles are kept broad where a more precise title is unavailable. Unknown start/end dates, ordering and missing education details are explicitly identified; they are not inferred to fill a timeline.

## Images and independence

Portrait files and retrieval credits are recorded in `../assets/images/industry-profiles/sources.json`. Public creator portraits were downloaded at the site owner's express request. Public availability and attribution do not establish a reuse license. Profiles without a selected portrait use locally generated initials avatars. Portrait and avatar links lead to the corresponding profile or LinkedIn page, depending on context.

Every page states that featured people are independent of Jaipur Engineers and are not presented as its employees, faculty, mentors or endorsers. The section is also independent of LinkedIn.

## Maintenance

Before updating a role or follower count, review the person's exact LinkedIn identity and a current primary source. Keep the original eight routes stable. Update the source URL and review context together, preserve the independence statement, and do not imply a complete career history when only partial public information is available.
